# Tick Saver

A frame-saver / input buffer. Press **F6** in game to cycle the tick save: off, 1, 2, 4, 8 ticks.

- Early presses are held and applied when you land or touch an orb within N ticks
- Optional hold extension: releases are delayed by N ticks
- Overlay shows the tick save, the ticks last used, and the total for the attempt

Do not use this on records you submit to lists or leaderboards.
